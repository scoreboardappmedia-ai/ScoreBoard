## 2. Data Collected
ScoreBoard may process the following data categories through its membership system while providing live scores, match statistics, and football content:

### 2.1. Account and Profile Data
Under the membership system:

* Email address
* Username / Nickname
* Profile photo (if uploaded by the user)

### 2.2. Usage and Device Information
* Device type (phone/tablet, operating system, OS version)
* Application version
* IP address (for log records and security purposes)
* In-app usage statistics (how often certain pages are used, etc.)
* Crash reports and performance data (via Google Crashlytics)
* Usage analytics (via Google Analytics)

### 2.3. Location Information
ScoreBoard may process your location information for the following purposes:

* **Time Zone Detection:** To display match times and content according to your local time zone.
* **Local Content Provision:** To provide news and content based on your region.
* **Statistical Analysis:** For usage statistics and analytical purposes (via Google Analytics).
* **Error Reporting:** For detecting and resolving application errors (via Google Crashlytics).

Your location information is considered sensitive data and is processed only for the purposes specified above and in a secure manner. (Usually processed at city or country level of precision.)

### 2.4. Notification and Preference Data
* Preferences regarding which team, league, or match you wish to receive notifications for.
* Selection of notification type (goal, red card, match start/end, etc.).
* Device-specific notification token (technical identifier required for Firebase Cloud Messaging delivery).

### 2.5. Search Data
ScoreBoard uses the Algolia service for content search. Your search queries and search history may be processed by Algolia.

* To provide search service: Content search and personalization of search results (via Algolia).
* Advertising and Marketing activities: Displaying personalized advertisements based on your explicit consent or our legitimate interest.
* Security and prevention of misuse: Detection of suspicious account activities, prevention of fraud and misuse attempts, storage of log records.
* Fulfillment of legal obligations: Tax and accounting obligations, responding to legitimate requests from official authorities.
